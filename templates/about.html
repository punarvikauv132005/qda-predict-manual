About the Project

Understanding aviation risk prediction with QDA

What is Aviation Accident Prediction?

Aviation accident prediction is the process of using historical flight data, weather conditions and aircraft parameters to estimate the probability that a particular flight will encounter an accident or safety incident.

Instead of reacting after a problem occurs, predictive systems allow airlines, pilots and ground staff to take proactive safety measures — delaying a flight, adjusting speed, or performing additional inspections — before a risky situation develops.

  • Proactive risk mitigation
  • Combines aircraft, pilot & weather factors
  • Supports operational decision-making
  • Improves overall flight safety culture
Why It Matters

Every year, weather and mechanical factors contribute to thousands of flight incidents. A reliable early-warning system can be the difference between a routine flight and an emergency.

What is Quadratic Discriminant Analysis?

The Method

QDA is a supervised classification algorithm that assumes each class follows a multivariate Gaussian (normal) distribution. Unlike Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA), QDA allows every class to have its own covariance matrix, producing curved (quadratic) decision boundaries.

For each feature vector x, QDA computes the probability that x belongs to each class using Bayes' theorem, then assigns the sample to the class with the highest posterior probability.

How QDA Works
  1. Estimate the mean vector for each risk class.
  2. Estimate the covariance matrix for each risk class.
  3. Compute the quadratic discriminant function for every class.
  4. Calculate the posterior probability of each risk level.
  5. Assign the flight to the class with the highest probability.
  6. Return the confidence score as the prediction probability.

Hybrid ML Ensemble (QDA + More)

Why an Ensemble?

Single classifiers can be biased or overfit in different ways. This system trains four complementary classifiers on the same flight data and combines them with a soft-voting ensemble:

  • QDA — quadratic boundaries between risk classes.
  • Random Forest — robust to noise and captures feature interactions.
  • Gradient Boosting — strong accuracy on structured data.
  • Logistic Regression — fast, interpretable baseline with class balancing.
  • Soft-Voting Ensemble — averages the probability outputs of all four.

Each model also votes individually, and the result page shows the full model consensus so you can see whether all algorithms agree on the risk.

Why QDA Was Chosen

Handles Complex Boundaries

Flight risk is rarely linear. QDA's quadratic decision surfaces capture the non-linear relationships between weather and aircraft parameters.

Natural Probabilities

QDA outputs a genuine probability for each risk class, giving pilots and dispatchers an honest confidence score — not just a label.

Fast & Interpretable

QDA trains and predicts in milliseconds on modest hardware, making it ideal for real-time, in-flight risk assessment.

Project Objectives

Classify flight risk into Low, Medium and High using a QDA + ML ensemble.

Include manually entered weather and in-flight dynamics parameters.

Detect accident probability with hard safety signatures per flight.

Provide targeted safety measures driven by the top risk contributors.
